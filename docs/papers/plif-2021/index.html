01 / SCIENTIFIC ORIENTATION

Research question and method

Editorial synthesis from the original publication. These descriptions are not the verbatim abstract, or independent reproduction.

Question

Can a membrane time constant be learned jointly with synaptic weights?

Method

Parametric leaky integrate-and-fire (PLIF) units make neuronal time constants learnable during gradient-based training.

Evaluation described by the authors

Static MNIST, Fashion-MNIST, CIFAR-10 and event datasets N-MNIST, CIFAR10-DVS, and DVS128 Gesture are evaluated in the authors' paper.
