# Community governance

**Status:** provisional governance for the initial setup phase (October 2026). This document describes the intended process, not an elected governing body or an established contributor council.

## Mission and scope

SNNCommunity is an independent, open, international community focused on spiking neural networks (SNNs), including education, algorithms, reproducibility, neuromorphic systems, and SNN-related embodied intelligence. Its work is neither an official university program nor an endorsement of particular academic papers, vendors, or projects.

## Roles

- **Organization owners** maintain GitHub access, security, membership, and organization-wide settings. Their accounts and privileges are managed through GitHub.
- **Repository maintainers** review contributions, maintain documentation and tests, and coordinate releases within their projects.
- **Contributors** propose changes, report issues, review research, and develop code. A formal membership or invitation is not required for public pull requests.
- **Participants** join public discussions and learning activities, following the Code of Conduct.

No additional maintainers or councils are claimed to have been appointed by this document.

## How decisions are made

1. Small documentation and maintenance changes are reviewed in pull requests.
2. New projects, major technical standards, and changes to governance start with a written proposal linked to an issue.
3. Maintainers summarize trade-offs, invite feedback where practical, and record decisions in issues or PRs.
4. Decisions affecting publication rights, funding, official affiliation, contributor licensing, or access privileges require explicit organization-owner review.
5. A rejected proposal may be revised or reconsidered when new evidence becomes available.

The default model during setup is **maintainer-led, publicly documented decision-making**. A more formal representative governance model may be proposed after there is a sustained, diverse contributor base.

## Review expectations

- Scientific claims must have traceable sources; experiments must document their protocols and limitations.
- Conflicts of interest and material affiliations should be disclosed when relevant.
- A maintainer must not self-certify an independent reproduction of their own experiment.
- Reviews address correctness, licenses, maintainability, accessibility, and safety.
- Nontrivial changes should normally receive a review by someone other than the author once a second maintainer is available. Until then, self-merged changes should be clearly attributed.

## Project boundaries and ownership

Personal and unpublished research remains with its authors. Sharing a link or participating in this community does not transfer copyright or imply permission to redistribute materials. Every software/data repository should publish its own license and contribution rules, as appropriate.

Organization-wide policies may be inherited by repositories that do not override them. Project-specific security reporting and scientific validation must be handled in the relevant repository.

## Community conduct and enforcement

Read [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md), [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), and [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md). GitHub owners can restrict abusive accounts or remove violating material. Any appeal may be requested via an issue that omits personal or sensitive details while a confidential contact method is being established.

## Amendments

Governance amendments require a publicly described proposal and organization-owner approval. Material updates should include a clear change summary and rationale.
